# The ask

Made up. Northwind Analytics is a fictional maker of finance software, and the guide, the people and every number here are invented.

The ask, as typed:

> Can you set up a LinkedIn campaign for our new guide, "The 5-day close"? It's for finance leaders at mid-size companies in the US and Canada. We have about 3,000 USD for November, starting November 3. We want to track downloads, and we'd like to see which message works best.

Answers to the skill's follow-up questions, given in 1 reply:

- **After the click:** download the guide. The landing page is https://www.northwind-analytics.example/guides/5-day-close, and a short form gates the PDF.
- **Who:** Finance and Accounting, Director and above, at companies with 201 to 5,000 employees, in the United States and Canada.
- **Budget and dates:** 3,000 USD at most, November 3 to November 30.
- **Conversion:** "Guide download". We just pointed it at this guide's thank-you page, so it has no history yet.
- **Facts the ads can use:** the guide is 12 pages; it has a checklist of 23 close tasks in the order that saves the most days; it's built from interviews with 40 controllers; it includes a close calendar template for days 1 to 5.
- **Off limits:** no customer names, and no claims about how fast customers close.
- **Format:** single image. Images get designed after the copy is approved.
- **Audiences page today** (Plan > Audiences): Customers - all contacts is Ready, Employees is Ready, Competitor employees is Expired.

The skill ran in Export mode with `linkedin-launch-defaults.json`, `forecast.csv` and `ad-set-performance-90d.csv` from this folder.
