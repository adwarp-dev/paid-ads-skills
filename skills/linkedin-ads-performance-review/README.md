# LinkedIn Ads performance review

LinkedIn Ads performance review with KPIs by campaign, ad set and ad (CTR, CPC, CPM, cost per lead or conversion, conversion rate), top and bottom performers against like-for-like peers, week over week direction, creative fatigue, and scale, pause, refresh and watch lists ranked by money at stake. Judgment floors (about 1,000 impressions or 3 results) keep small numbers from driving decisions. Works from Campaign Manager exports or live through Adwarp.

## Ask it

- "how are my LinkedIn ads doing"
- "LinkedIn ads performance review"
- "what should I scale or pause on LinkedIn"
- "which LinkedIn ads are working"
- "weekly LinkedIn ads check"
- "why did LinkedIn cost per lead go up"

## Install

```bash
npx skills add adwarp-dev/paid-ads-skills
```

Or copy this folder into `~/.claude/skills/` or your agent's skills folder.

## How it runs

From a CSV export with nothing connected, or live through [Adwarp](https://adwarp.dev), the free, open-source MCP server and CLI for LinkedIn Ads and Google Ads. Adwarp setup: [adwarp.dev/docs/install](https://adwarp.dev/docs/install).

More on adwarp.dev: [adwarp.dev/linkedin-ads/reporting](https://adwarp.dev/linkedin-ads/reporting), [adwarp.dev/linkedin-ads/optimization](https://adwarp.dev/linkedin-ads/optimization), [adwarp.dev/linkedin-ads](https://adwarp.dev/linkedin-ads).
